Free Historical Tennis Results and Betting Odds Data
26 years of results and match betting odds

Tennis-Data provides the tennis bettor with free computer-ready (Excel) tennis results and betting odds data to help with the development and analysis of tennis betting systems. In doing so Tennis-Data takes the time out of recompiling pages and pages of results data and past betting odds found on a number of tennis results and odds comparison websites.

Results for the men's ATP tour date back to January 2000, including Grand Slams, Masters Series, Masters Cup and International Series competitions. Historical head-to-head betting odds go back to 2001. And from 2007 results and historical betting odds for the women's WTA tour as well! All data files can be accessed below, one Excel file per calendar season. A set of Notes describes what can be found in the data. Data for the current season is updated weekly at the end of each weekly round of ATP and WTA tournaments (2 weeks after Grand Slams).

Tennis-Data maintains full copyright over the data files in their spreadsheet format. Its use is intended for private individuals only, not commerical or data training products using automated bots/scrapers/AI. If you have any other questions about either the data or how it can be used for your own betting system, just contact Tennis-Data.
